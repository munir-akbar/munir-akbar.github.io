About

Building websites that look great — and perform even better.

I work at the intersection of WordPress development and technical SEO — building sites that are fast out of the gate and structured to be found. That means clean, semantic markup, deliberate performance budgets, and custom functionality when the page builder isn't enough.

Every project moves through the same lens: does this help a real visitor find the site, understand it quickly, and take action? Frontend polish, backend architecture, and search visibility are treated as one connected system, not separate line items.
